Uncorked – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Uncorked ("the app") is a wine recommendation app that helps you choose wine in restaurants and at home. It is currently offered as a pilot to a small group of invited testers. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and your choices.

Who is responsible for your data

David Dalton, Dublin, Ireland ("we"), is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: support@uncorkedwineapp.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password (stored in a protected, hashed form).
  • Your taste profile: your answers to the onboarding questions, your wine preferences, and your budget ranges.
  • Your wine activity: wines you add to your cellar, wines you like or rate, and the recommendations and pairings you request.
  • Photos you choose to submit: pictures of wine labels, wine lists and food menus. Photos are used only to read the wines, dishes and prices on them.
  • Survey answers and feedback you give during the pilot.
  • Basic technical data: such as app version and error logs, used to keep the service working.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or payment details, and we do not use advertising trackers.

How we use it

  • To create your account and provide wine and pairing recommendations.
  • To read the photos you submit and add the wines and dishes they show to the app. Wine lists and menus you submit may be shown to other users of the app after review, but never together with your name or email.
  • To understand how well the app works during the pilot and improve it.

Our legal basis is performing the service you asked for (your account) and our legitimate interest in improving the app. Where we ask for your consent, such as for access to your camera or photos, you can withdraw it at any time in your phone's settings.

Who processes your data

  • Amazon Web Services hosts the app and its database in Ireland (EU).
  • Anthropic provides the AI that reads photos and text and writes wine descriptions and recommendations. Relevant content (such as the photos you submit and your preferences) is sent to Anthropic to produce results for you. It is not used by us to identify you.

We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while you have an account and delete it when you delete your account. Photos submitted for review are kept until they are approved or rejected, and rejected photos are deleted. Pilot survey answers may be kept in anonymised form after the pilot.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive a copy of it. You can delete your account and its data at any time from inside the app. You can also email support@uncorkedwineapp.com. You have the right to complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie) or your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is for adults of legal drinking age in their country. It is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and tell you in the app.

Please drink responsibly.
